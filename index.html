Admissions

Start Your Journey at NATO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Complete a simple application and join a school committed to academic excellence, values formation, and student support.

Application Steps

  1. Submit your online inquiry or visit the school office.
  2. Prepare your report card and PSA birth certificate.
  3. Attend the placement interview and orientation.

Admission Requirements

  • Certified true copy of grade report
  • PSA birth certificate or registered baptismal certificate
  • School endorsement letter (if applicable)

Questions?

Contact the admissions office for help with requirements, schedules, and scholarship guidance.
