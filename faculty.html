Dedicated Educators

Each member of our faculty is committed to building a supportive classroom, encouraging student growth, and creating a strong school community.

Teachers inspires learners to write, read, and reflect critically and explore the wonders of the natural world through inquiry, experimentation, and hands-on learning.

Mathematics and Physical Education Teachers

Filipino Teacher nurtures students' appreciation of the Filipino language, literature, and culture. Through meaningful learning experiences, students develop effective communication skills, critical thinking, and a deeper sense of national identity and pride.
