Senior High School (SHS) Courses / Tracks

Our Senior High School program offers a
variety of tracks designed to help students
discover their interests, develop their skills,
and prepare for higher education, employment,
entrepreneurship, or future careers.

Each track provides specialized subjects
and hands-on learning experiences aligned with
the Department of Education(DepEd) curriculum.
