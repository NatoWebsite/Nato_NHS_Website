Admissions

Become Part of a Safe, Caring, and Future-Ready Learning Community.

Our admissions process is designed to welcome students and families who want Quality Basic Education, Values Formation, and Opportunities for Growth.

HOW TO ENROLL

Follow these steps to complete your admission and begin the next chapter of your learning journey.

Step 1: Inquiry

Reach out to our admissions office or visit the campus to learn about schedules, requirements, and school programs.

Step 2: Submit documents

  • Grade report or evaluation form
  • PSA birth certificate
  • Medical certificate and photo ID

Step 3: Orientation

Attend the student orientation session and get to know the school community, programs, and campus resources.

Admission Requirements

These items help us ensure every new student starts with the support they need.

New Student Form

Complete the application form and submit it with the required documents.

Official Records

Bring the latest report card and certificate of transfer or completion if coming from another school.

Family Support

Parents and guardians are welcome to discuss financial aid, scholarships, or learner support programs.
