Vision, Mission & Values

We Prepare Learners for Success, Service, and Leadership.

NATO National High School is committed to providing Quality, Equitable, and Culture-Based Education in a Child-Friendly, Gender-Sensitive, and Safe Environment.

What We Stand For

Vision

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education.

Mission

Students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment while teachers facilitate learning and nurture every learner.

Core Values

Our Core Values are Maka-Diyos (God-centered), Maka-tao (People-oriented), Makakalikasan (Nature-oriented), and Makabansa (Nationalistic).
