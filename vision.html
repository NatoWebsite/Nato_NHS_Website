<b>Vision</b>

Vision

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality,equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education.

Mission

Students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment, while teachers facilitate learning and nurture every learner Department of Education.

Core Values

The core values include Maka-Diyos (God-centered), Maka-tao (People-oriented), Makakalikasan (Nature-oriented), and Makabansa (Nationalistic).
